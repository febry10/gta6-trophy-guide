Leonida Companion is a fully offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5: all 55 official trophies with their published names, descriptions and grades, each with an original guide written for this app — what to do, where and how, and when it can be missed — that names its sources (PowerPyx, GTABase and cross-checks) per trophy. The tracker saves your checklist in the browser with export and import, filters by kind and grade plus search, offers a "Next up" pointer driven by the roadmap, hides story spoilers until you earn them, and carries a tick-off sub-checklist for the 18 meditation spots. The Roadmap tab lays out the three stages to the platinum with live per-stage progress and the cited estimates (difficulty 4/10, ~200 hours, one playthrough, no mission replay), and the Missables tab gives the honest, sourced verdict: exactly one trophy is possibly missable — the Chapter 1 meditation behind Positive Mental Attitude — plus a short watch list, while a Sources & honesty page separates what is official from what is ours and says plainly which five trophies no guide has confirmed yet. No accounts, no network, no Rockstar artwork: everything runs from its own static files.
## Run it
```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
